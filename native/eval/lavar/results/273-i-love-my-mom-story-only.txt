My mom’s name is Lee. She works hard all the time.

 And I learn so much from her!

When my mom cooks, I learn to cook too.

When she waters the flowers, I learn to water the flowers too.

When she washes our clothes, I learn to wash our clothes too.

When she dries our clothes, I learn to dry our clothes too.

My mom also bought me a bicycle, and she taught me how to ride it.

As I ride my bicycle, my mom smiles when I go “WHEEEEEEE!”

At night, my mom makes the bed. I learn how to make the bed too.

Before bedtime, my mom tells me stories.

When I'm sleepy, my mom sings me a lullaby.

I love my mom so much!
