LEOTA FLORENCE BREDESON, 92, of Honolulu, died Dec. 5, 2000. Born in Barbour County, W. Va. A retired beautician and seamstress. Survived by son, Roland; daughter, Lori Arizumi; five grandchildren; great-granddaughter; sister, Retha Mockus. Visitation 5:30 p.m. Sunday at First Baptist Church of Honolulu; service 6 p.m. Aloha attire.
