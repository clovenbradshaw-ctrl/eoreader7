HELEN 'HARUNO' TAGUCHI, 88, of Mililani, died Dec. 5, 2000. Born in Pahala, Hawaii. Retired as a minister with Tenrikyo Church of Hawaii. Visitation Thursday at Oahu Cemetery chapel; service noon. Burial 1 p.m. at Hawaii State Veterans Cemetery. No flowers. Casual attire. Arrangements by Leeward Funeral Home.
