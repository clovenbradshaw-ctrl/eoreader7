EMMA M. BULLUCK. NEWPORT NEWS - Emma Mae McLawhorn Bulluck, 81, a former Hampton resident, died Tuesday, Dec. 8. Survived by two sons, Adrian Bulluck and Kenneth M. Bulluck of Hampton; a daughter, Linda Bulluck of the home; two grandchildren and two great-grandchildren. Visitation from 7 to 9 p.m. Thursday at Wilkerson Funeral Home. Funeral 2 p.m. Friday at the funeral home by the Rev. Gene Asbill. Burial in Winterville Cemetery.
JOSEPH CRANFORD. WILLIAMSBURG - Joseph de Ronde Cranford, 86, a Washington, D.C., native, died Sunday, Dec. 6. Mr. Cranford was a highway construction superintendent for Corson and Gruman, now C.G. Enterprises Inc. He was a founding partner of the Asphalt Services Co. and attended Lafayette College. Survived by a son, Page D. Cranford of Virginia Beach; and two grandchildren. Memorial service 2:30 p.m. Monday at St. John's Episcopal Church, Chevy Chase, Md. Burial will be private. Memorials to James City County Rescue Squad.
