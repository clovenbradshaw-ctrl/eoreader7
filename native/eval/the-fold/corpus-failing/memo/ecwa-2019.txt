ERIE COUNTY WATER AUTHORITY INTEROFFICE MEMORANDUM. March 18, 2019. To: Terrence D. McCracken, Secretary to the Authority. From: Leonard F. Kowalski, Senior Distribution Engineer. Subject: Contract GP-007 Water System Improvements Village of Depew and Town of West Seneca. Attached is the Recommendation to Award for the above referenced project. The following documents are attached: the ECWA Authorization Form, the Recommendation for Award of Contract, the Consultant's Recommendation letter, and the Bid Tab. The consultant performed the mathematical check of bids and verified that the low bidder for this contract is CMH Company, Inc. The award amount is $2,487,490.00. This contractor has performed other work for the Authority in the past in a competent manner. Based on the information that was provided above, it was my recommendation to Russ Stoll, Executive Engineer that the GP-007 project be awarded to CMH Company, Inc.
