Interoffice Memorandum. January 31, 2014. TO: Mayor Teresa Jacobs and the Board of County Commissioners. FROM: Johnny M. Richardson, Manager, Procurement Division. SUBJECT: Consultant Selection, RFP Y14-901-CH Continuing Professional Consulting Construction Engineering and Inspection Services. RECOMMENDATION. Selection of one firm and an alternate to provide Continuing Professional Consulting Construction Engineering and Inspection Services, Request for Proposals Y14-901-CH. Further request the Board authorize execution of the final contract by the Procurement Division to establish hourly rates for future requirements. The contract's performance period will be for one year with two one-year renewals. PURPOSE. To select a firm to provide continuing consulting for construction engineering and inspection services. DISCUSSION. The consultant will provide project management and support services to oversee the construction of a variety of County transportation and roadway construction projects. Services will include engineering and inspection services, coordination with contractors, shop drawing reviews, evaluation and negotiation of change orders, and review of pay requests. The proposals of Rummel, Klepper and Kahl and Tierra scored too low for Board consideration.
