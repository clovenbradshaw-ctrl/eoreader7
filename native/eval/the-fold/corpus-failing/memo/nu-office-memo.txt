Office Memo. An interoffice memorandum or memo is an internal document written to inform employees of the company or organization of policy, procedures, announcements, events, or to give instructions. An interoffice memo includes the following sections: Header labeled Interoffice Memorandum, To identify who should read the document, From identify who is sending the document, Date identify the current date of the memo, Subject identify the nature of the memo. Body communicate a message or idea while providing facts about the subject, formatted in block paragraphs. Paragraph 1: Introduction provide the background of the issue, problem, or main idea. Paragraph 2: The Facts include the facts. Paragraph 3: The Solution be concise yet informative. Paragraph 4: The Conclusion includes a summary and a call to action.
