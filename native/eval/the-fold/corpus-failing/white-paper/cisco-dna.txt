The Cisco Digital Network Architecture Vision - An Overview. White Paper. Table of Contents: Introduction, Requirements for Enterprise Networks in the Digital Age, DNA Tenets, Digital Network Architecture Overview, Resilience and Security Considerations, Conclusion. As enterprise business processes become increasingly digitized, new demands on the enterprise network architecture arise. This overview presents the vision for Cisco Digital Network Architecture. The architecture is built to facilitate fast and flexible network services that support digitalized business processes. ENTERPRISE network architectures are being challenged by the evolution of digital businesses that are embracing mobile, cloud, video, and Internet-of-Things technologies. This paper is written for CXOs, network architects, and network engineers who wish to understand the technical details underpinning DNA. Engineering-minded decision makers who want to understand how the architecture supports the business benefits of DNA may also benefit from this paper. CONCLUSION. Ensuring the availability of network services continues to be a baseline requirement.
