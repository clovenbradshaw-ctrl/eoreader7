Vocality White Paper - VCOM Media Gateway. Author Frank Corsney. Table of Contents: Introduction, Problem Statement, Proposed Solution, Introduction of Solution, Application of Solution, Deployment Scenarios, Conclusion. INTRODUCTION. This White Paper describes unified communications challenges and how a media gateway can help to overcome these challenges. PROBLEM STATEMENT. Quick and efficient communications result in cost saving and more importantly lives. PROPOSED SOLUTION. VCOM, by Intracom Systems, is a multi-access communications and tactical intercom and conferencing solution for professional and mission critical applications. VCOM acts as the central connectivity point for all communications between radios, Voice over IP networks and cellular phones. VCOM ties all the divergent platforms together in one cohesive unified communications system allowing all users to communicate with each other seamlessly. CONCLUSION. VCOM, when combined with Vocality's BASICS Radio Relay, provides a cost-effective solution for operationally challenging networks. The ability to seamlessly tie various forms of communication into one cohesive network allows the timely dissemination of information.
