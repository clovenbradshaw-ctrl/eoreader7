An encyclopedia is a reference work or compendium providing summaries of knowledge, either general or special, in a particular field or discipline. The term encyclopedia comes from the Greek enkyklos paideia, literally the things of boys in a circle, meaning a general knowledge. In ancient times encyclopedias were teaching tools for instruction of the aristocracy. The encyclopedia as we recognize it today was developed from the dictionary in the eighteenth century. An encyclopedia treats each subject in more depth and conveys the most relevant accumulated knowledge on that subject, given the overall length of the particular work. Encyclopedias can be general, containing articles on topics in every field, or specialized. Projects such as Wikipedia are examples of new forms of the encyclopedia as information retrieval becomes simpler.
