History is the study of the past, particularly the people, events, and phenomena of earlier times. The term history comes from the Greek historia, meaning inquiry or knowledge acquired by investigation. Traditionally, the study of history has been considered a part of the humanities, alongside a subject such as literature. History involves asking whether alternative accounts might tell a different story, or whether the account contains any bias. Because history is such a large subject, most historical studies have specialized on a particular subject. The Indus Valley Civilization offers examples of what is called identity or cultural politics when alternative accounts of history are offered to counter the allegation of bias.
