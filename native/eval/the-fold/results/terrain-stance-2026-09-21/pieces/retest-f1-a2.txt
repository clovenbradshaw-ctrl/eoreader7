The Office of Internal Audit, in its comprehensive audit of the Metropolitan Nashville Homeless Impact Division, issued a report on March 30, 2023, identifying several areas for improvement. The audit found that the Coordinated Entry policy and procedure manual was not fully updated, requiring continued effort to incorporate all necessary policies and procedures. Outstanding action: continue the update process for the Coordinated Entry policy and procedure manual to incorporate all required policies and procedures, with a revised implementation date of September 2, 2025. Recommendation C.1, on establishing an encampment management team and plan: the Homeless Impact Division completed a community Encampment Outdoor Housing Plan. This plan was developed through extensive community engagement, involving partners from various sectors, including Metro departments, committee leaders, and individuals with lived experience. This information highlights the audit's focus on improving the effectiveness of the Office of Homeless Services and the commitment of the committee to ensuring the best possible outcomes for those experiencing homelessness.

The Office of Internal Audit's monitoring and follow-up process is essential for ensuring that high- and medium-risk recommendations are implemented effectively, leading to the intended results. Status: Partially Implemented. Recommendation D.1, on ensuring access to the Homeless Management Information System is granted only after new users have completed all necessary steps and an end-user license agreement is properly executed: a random sample of users was selected from a list of all users granted access for the period from 10/01/2022 to 11/30/2024, and supporting end-user license agreements were reviewed. Note: the Homeless Impact Division was formerly a part of Social Services. Of the seven recommendations, six were fully implemented and one was partially implemented. Another recommendation, D.2, focused on obtaining annual SOC 2 Type 2 reports from WellSky to ensure data security and availability, as the Office of Homeless Services is currently working on another update to address the remaining exceptions.

The audit's objectives were to assess whether the recommended actions, or an acceptable alternative, had been implemented by June 30, 2025. The Office of Homeless Services has been implementing the recommendations, using the Strategic Plan Tracker as a key tool for monitoring progress. This tracker, reviewed and updated monthly in the All Chair Subcommittee meeting, provides a comprehensive overview of the Office's activities, tasks, responsible parties, notes, and proposed timelines for completion. The audit's findings have been valuable in driving positive change within the Office of Homeless Services. Recommendation B.1, on updating the policies and procedures manual to include all required Housing and Urban Development policies and procedures or document the reason for exclusion: the Coordinated Entry policies and procedures were updated.
