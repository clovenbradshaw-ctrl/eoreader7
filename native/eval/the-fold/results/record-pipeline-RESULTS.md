# The sixth medium: the instrument's own record, through its own pipeline — 2026-09-02

*Driver: `eval/record-pipeline.mjs`, re-runnable against
`record/explore-record.jsonl` as it stands. The first run of `signal.js`
IN ANGER — no planted grammar, no score line: 3,083 real rows of what
this instrument actually did over three weeks, with whatever beats the
search-aware ceiling reported as a genuine discovery and nothing
surviving reported as a measured absence.*

## Design

- **Sources:** the record's own two operating periods (week 1 vs week 2+)
  — genuinely different work being done, the closest this medium has to
  independent runs of "the instrument operating".
- **Vocabulary:** the record's own event names at ≥10 occurrences —
  enumerated, a mechanical fact about the file, not a search.
- **Instruments:** three same-scope, different-rule phrasings of "what
  happened together" (same clock-minute; >60s-silence gaps; fixed-12
  windows) — the spatial run's own law applied deliberately.
- **Alphabet shape:** heavy-tailed (web-fetch is 60% of all rows) — the
  exact small-alphabet/dominant-symbol territory where turbulence found
  the unlicensed share floor. The search-aware ceiling landed at **0.846**
  — the null arm earning its keep precisely where that run predicted.

## Findings (all four verifiable against the app's own documented flow)

| finding | share | standing |
|---|---|---|
| `read-start ← source-open` | 1.000 | 1 source, 3/3 instruments |
| `read-reused ← source-open` | 1.000 | 1 source, 3/3 instruments |
| `read-done ← read-start` | 0.950 | 1 source, 3/3 instruments |
| `server-start` opens phrases | 0.867 | **CORROBORATED — 2/2 sources, 2/3 instruments** |

Every one is TRUE of the instrument by construction — opening a source
starts a read (or reuses a memoized one), a read that starts finishes,
and a server boot begins an operating burst. The pipeline discovered its
host's own architecture from its behavioural residue, taught nothing.

The three one-source findings are honest drift, not weakness: the read
pipeline's traffic is concentrated in week 1 (later weeks were eval- and
web-driven), so week 2+ genuinely offers too few read events to
corroborate. The counts say so rather than the findings being inflated.
