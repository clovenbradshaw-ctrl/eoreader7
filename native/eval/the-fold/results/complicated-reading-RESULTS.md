# A genuinely complicated text, read mechanically — the reasoning shown

**Driver:** `eval/complicated-reading.mjs` (re-runnable; live-fetches its
material). **Material:** the Second Schleswig War — the shooting half of
the Schleswig-Holstein question, Palmerston's "only three people ever
understood it — one is dead, one went mad, and I have forgotten it."
48,349 chars fetched live from en.wikipedia.org, 2026-08-29. A second run
over the shorter "Schleswig-Holstein question" article itself (13,531
chars) produced the same shape of trace at smaller scale and is not
separately tabled here.

**What this demonstrates:** the P65 capability cells doing real work in
sequence on real material, each stage printing its verdicts AND its typed
refusals — no model call anywhere, every number mechanical. The honest
mistakes are kept in this document on purpose: showing the reasoning
includes showing where it is wrong and why, in the mechanism's own terms.

**Stage 2's pronoun rung — two real bindings at the declared numbers

not composed into this driver). The binding is the recall physics working
correctly over an undeclared type constraint. Named as the next
declaration this driver should receive, not smoothed over.

## What to take from it

1. **The ladder composes**: presence → establishment → stated edges →
   separation → induced kinds, each stage consuming the previous stage's
   admissions and refusals, every verdict carrying its evidence.
2. **The refusals are load-bearing**: 258 below-floor refusals are what
   keep the belief graph and the kinds field clean enough for the null to
   mean something.
3. **The wrong answers are typed, not hidden**: both pronoun bindings
   expose an undeclared constraint the organ itself documents — the
   failure names its own fix.
4. **Months establish as referents** (`april`, `august`, `december`
   cleared the floor): recurrence is not personhood, and nothing in this
   driver claims it is — individuation typing (P40) is the organ that
   would say so, and composing it in is real, named, unattempted work.
