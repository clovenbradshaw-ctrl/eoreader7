The Pyramid of Austerlitz

The only pyramid on European soil is situated in the village of Woudenberg.

The Pyramid of Austerlitz is a relic from a time when the Netherlands, under French rule, was subject to massive changes, which still echo through in the present time. Matters such as surnames, the tax system and a registry of births, deaths and marriages have all been introduced by Napoleon Bonaparte.
