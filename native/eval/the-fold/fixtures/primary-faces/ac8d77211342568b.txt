Title: Long-term Orbit Stability of the Apollo 11 Eagle Lunar Module Ascent Stage

Authors: James Meador

View a PDF of the paper titled Long-term Orbit Stability of the Apollo 11 Eagle Lunar Module Ascent Stage, by James Meador

View PDF
HTML (experimental)

Abstract: The Apollo 11 Eagle Lunar Module ascent stage was abandoned in lunar orbit after the historic landing in 1969. Its fate is unknown. Numerical analysis described here provides evidence that this object might have remained in lunar orbit to the present day. The simulations show a periodic variation in eccentricity of the orbit, correlated to the selenographic longitude of the apsidal line. The rate of apsidal precession is correlated to eccentricity. These two factors appear to interact to stabilize the orbit over the long term.

13 pages, 8 figures. Submitted to Planetary and Space Science
