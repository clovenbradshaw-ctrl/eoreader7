Apollo 11 Restored EVA Part 1

Pizza Marko

This clip is the first hour of the Apollo 11 Moonwalk, beginning at the time the television was switched on. This clip was restored and enhanced by NASA and Lowry Digital from the best possible sources. Unfortunately those sources were the post-scan-converted images, as the telemetry tapes containing the slow-scan television was not located and is believed to have been erased.

For more information on the Apollo television broadcasts, see our original documentary Live From the Moon, available on DVD and Blu-ray at www.spacecraftfilms.com
