The RMS Titanic struck an iceberg on the night of April 14, 1912, and sank in the early hours of April 15. The ship was on its maiden voyage from Southampton to New York City. The Titanic killed about 1,500 people, out of more than 2,200 aboard. The Titanic was traveling at high speed when it struck the iceberg. The ship carried 2,223 passengers and crew on that crossing. Captain Edward Smith was in command of the vessel when it sank. The ship was built at the Harland and Wolff shipyard in Belfast, Ireland. The Titanic was designed to carry 3,547 people, with twenty lifeboats on board. Distress rockets were fired from the sinking ship beginning at 12:45 a.m. The Carpathia rescued 705 survivors from the lifeboats on the morning of April 15. The wreck of the Titanic was discovered in 1985 at a depth of 3,800 meters.
