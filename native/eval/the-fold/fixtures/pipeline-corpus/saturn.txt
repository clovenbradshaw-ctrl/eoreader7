Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest planet in the Solar System, after Jupiter. It is famous for its prominent ring system, made mostly of ice particles with a smaller amount of rocky debris and dust. Saturn has dozens of known moons. Titan is Saturn's largest moon. A year on Saturn lasts about 29 Earth years, since Saturn takes 10,759 Earth days to complete one orbit. Saturn is about 1.4 billion kilometers from the Sun. The planet rotates very quickly, completing a full spin in about 10.7 hours. Saturn has a density lower than water, meaning it would float in a large enough ocean. The rings of Saturn extend to a width of about 282,000 kilometers. The planet was first observed through a telescope by Galileo Galilei in the year 1610. Titan has a thick atmosphere mostly of nitrogen and rivers of liquid methane on its surface.
