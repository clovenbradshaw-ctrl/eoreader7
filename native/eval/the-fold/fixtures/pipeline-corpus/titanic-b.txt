The Titanic sank on April 15, 1912, after colliding with an iceberg the night before. Investigators later estimated that 1,635 people lost their lives when the ship went down. The Titanic killed about 1,635 people, making it one of the deadliest peacetime maritime disasters in history. The Titanic was not traveling at high speed when it struck the iceberg. The official British inquiry reported that 1,517 people died in the disaster. The American inquiry concluded that the ship was going too fast for the ice conditions. One witness, surviving second officer Charles Lightoller, testified that the ship turned away from the iceberg just before impact. Another witness, fireman Frederick Barrett, testified that the collision tore a three-hundred-foot gash along the starboard side.
