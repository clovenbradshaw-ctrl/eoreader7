On March 30, 2023, the Office of Internal Audit issued the Audit of the Metropolitan Nashville Homeless Impact Division. The audit report included seven recommendations. All seven recommendations were accepted by management for implementation. The Office of Internal Audit guidelines require monitoring and follow-up to ensure that the recommendations assessed as high or medium risk are appropriately considered, effectively implemented, and yield intended results.

The objectives of this follow-up audit were to determine if the recommended actions or an acceptable alternative were implemented as of June 30, 2025. The scope of the follow-up audit included the seven accepted recommendations that management reported as implemented. Note: the Homeless Impact Division was formerly a part of Social Services. Since the audit, the Office of Homeless Services was created and is the owner of the recommendations.

Of the seven recommendations, six were fully implemented and one was partially implemented. Management should continue efforts to implement the one remaining partially implemented recommendation.

The initial audit report encompassed governance and oversight of the Homelessness Planning Council, Homeless Management Information System usage, and internal controls over access to homelessness data between January 1, 2020, and December 31, 2021. To achieve the follow-up audit objectives, auditors interviewed management regarding implementation efforts, reviewed relevant SOC 2 Type 2 documentation, reviewed Homeless Management Information System users and end-user license agreements, reviewed Homelessness Planning Council and Executive Planning meeting minutes, reviewed newly established policies, procedures, and plans, evaluated internal controls currently in place, and considered risk of fraud, waste, and abuse and information technology risks.

Recommendation A.1, on coordinating with the Homelessness Planning Council to keep the Strategic Community Plan relevant through periodic reviews of goals, benchmarks, and monitoring activities: the strategic plan refresh was completed for July 2023 through June 2026. The Strategic Plan Tracker is reviewed and updated in the All Chair Subcommittee meeting that occurs monthly, and includes activities, tasks, status, groups responsible by task, notes, and a proposed timeline for completion. Status: Implemented.

Recommendation B.1, on updating the policies and procedures manual to include all required Housing and Urban Development policies and procedures or document the reason for exclusion: the Coordinated Entry policies and procedures were updated. However, 5 of the 11 (45 percent) originally identified required policies and procedures remain exceptions. The Office of Homeless Services is currently working on another update to address the remaining exceptions. Status: Partially Implemented. Outstanding action: continue the update process for the Coordinated Entry policy and procedure manual to incorporate all required policies and procedures, with a revised implementation date of September 2, 2025.

Recommendation C.1, on establishing an encampment management team and plan: the Homeless Impact Division completed a community Encampment Outdoor Housing Plan. This plan included input from community partners, multiple Metro departments, committee leaders, and people with lived experience. Status: Implemented.

Recommendation D.1, on ensuring access to the Homeless Management Information System is granted only after new users have completed all necessary steps and an end-user license agreement is properly executed: a random sample of users was selected from a list of all users granted access for the period from 10/01/2022 to 11/30/2024, and supporting end-user license agreements were reviewed. Results of the sample may be projected to the population. Status: Implemented.

Recommendation D.2, on annually requesting a SOC 2 Type 2 report from WellSky to ensure data is secure and always available: SOC 2 Type 2 reports were obtained, and supporting documentation was reviewed to confirm. Status: Implemented.

Recommendation E.1, on establishing a process for periodic completion and review of the Housing and Urban Development resources that improve system administration and software vendor oversight: procedures were developed and implemented, and supporting documentation was reviewed. Status: Implemented.

Recommendation F.1, on documenting and retaining meeting minutes for all Homelessness Planning Council and Executive Planning meetings: procedures were developed and implemented. A random sample of meetings was selected for review of associated meeting minutes, and results of the sample can be projected to the population. Status: Implemented.
