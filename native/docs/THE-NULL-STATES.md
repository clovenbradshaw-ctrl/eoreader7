## Why the nulls are the wisdom

Every organ here perceives by difference from a nothing it rebuilds (SEED; eo-constitution II.8). So the instrument's whole vocabulary of *what it knows* is a vocabulary of *what it built as nothing and what failed to clear it*. A positive finding is one shape; a null is twenty-seven. `nul/index.js`'s own header says it in one line: every gap type is the same act at a different grain — refusing a claim. The ways-of-knowing register names the spoke: APOPHASIS, knowing-the-absence, whose validity condition is that the refusal REACHED its object, and whose corruption is silence read as anything at all.

This document does two things. It places every null the code already declares at its cell, so a new organ finds the null it owes before writing one. And it names the cells whose null is not yet declared — which, in a project that perceives only by difference, is exactly where the next capacity is.

## The laws every null obeys

1. **A null names its scope.** What was looked at — the extent, the pool, the cursor, the draws. A gap without a denominator is a verdict wearing a count (P66: "a handful of gaps stood in for a hundred chances"). `regime`, `unread_extent`, `pool`, `draws` are the scope fields.
2. **A null names what would fill it.** Its own REC trigger: a void re-zeros on one arrival; a cut is contested by a link; `unheard` fills when the vocabulary grows; `unread_extent` fills as the cursor moves; `fixture_absent` fills when the checkout does. A null that cannot say what would cancel it is not a null, it is a shrug.
3. **A null is a finding only when the search reached its object.** "Nothing beat the ceiling — a measured absence, not a failure to look" is a result; "the driver never ran the arm" is a fact about the reader (P41, P90). The two must never share a word.
4. **A null never convicts.** An organ may say "I have nothing to compare against"; it may never manufacture the second out of the first (the grounding ladder's constitutional statement; `beyond-reach` and `unheard` stay OFF the unsupported list).
5. **Nulls compose downward.** A null at Ground makes every figure over it a null — presupposition, not falsity (Strawson; `verification.js`'s domain order: a referent that fails to exist makes the Link and Lens cells typed GAPS, never falses).
6. **The mouth may relay a declared null and may never declare one.** "There is no mention of anything else" was the mouth declaring a void it never measured (P54). Every ∅ on the answer surface is an instrument's declaration with a scope, never the model's.

## What the grid says

**Two cells have no null.** SEG·Figure has no cut, and DEF·Ground has no void as an object. These are not oversights in a list; they are the two places the instrument cannot yet say "no" properly, and every measured failure of the last week sits on one of them: the denial read as a link with a sign (P43, the assay's wall 4a), the mouth declaring absence (P54), the witness's ∅ with no scope, the model's "no mention" standing where a measured void should. The cut is the geometry's no; the void is the calculus's no; and the contest is what the two make when they meet a link.

**Three negatives, three operators.** A cut (SEG) separates a subject from an extent and is disputable. A void (DEF) bounds an extent as empty and is re-zeroed by one arrival. A contest (CON·CONTESTED) relates a link to a cut. Reading all three as "contradicted" is why the reader has one verdict vocabulary for three acts.

**The pairing rule.** Every positive finding an organ can land has a null in the same cell, and the organ that lands the finding owes the null: an organ that binds (CON·Figure) owes `unbound`; an organ that composes (SYN·Figure) owes `withheld` and `vetoed` apart; an organ that reads on arrival (SIG·Ground) owes `unread_extent`; an organ that places a figure against a ground (NUL·Figure) owes the censored rank. A capacity registered without its null is a capacity that will read silence as support.

**The meta-null is the one to fear.** EVA·Figure's unreachable guard is the null of the instrument rather than of the material — a wall that is a comment. It is found only by asking whether the wall was ever reached (P88), and it is the failure shape this project has caught eleven times. Every null above is honest only if its own guard can fire; `record-log.test.mjs`'s mutated-entry control and `resolution.test.mjs`'s own first draft are what checking that looks like.

## What is not claimed

Coherence never establishes correspondence. A complete grid of nulls means the instrument can say "no" at every grain; it does not mean any "no" is true of the world. That null — the void of correspondence — has one filler, an oracle, and this document does not pretend to it.
