# The Ways of Knowing — the spokes, named, and the hub they point at

*Written 2026-09-02, at the user's direction: "consider how to name these
different ways of 'knowing', of different types of signal from noise that
point towards the central empty hub." Standing: **nomination** — the
census below is read off the code, and the code wins any disagreement.
Companion to THE-CORE-MECHANISM.md (which named three comparison
families) and THE-THREE-MATHEMATICS.md (the cube's structure); this
document is finer-grained than the first and orthogonal to the second.*

## Why name them at all

The word "checking" has been carrying at least nine different acts in
this project's conversations, the way "level" once carried two ladders
(LEVELS.md). Each act separates signal from noise **differently** — a
different thing plays the noise, a different thing survives — and the
project's worst measured errors were one way of knowing asked to do
another's job (the falsification probe asked a corpus to grant a license;
the naive Friston collapse read every gap as a correction). A shared
vocabulary is how a future session says "that's a triangulation question,
not a perturbation question" in five words instead of five paragraphs.

Every name below carries its **giver** — the received epistemology it
borrows from, named per this repo's own standing rule for received
vocabulary. Two traditions supply most of them: the Western one
(Popper, Whewell, Dawid, Wittgenstein), and the Sanskrit **pramāṇa**
tradition — the classical taxonomy of exactly this question, "by what
means is knowledge valid?" — whose fit is close enough in places
(anupalabdhi, śabda, vyāpti) that not citing it would be taking without
naming the giver.

## The census — nine spokes

*Nine is a count, not the cube's nine. No cell correspondence is claimed
and none was sought; forcing one would be numerology, and the census was
taken from code sites before it was counted.*

### 1. OSTENSION — knowing-where
**Signal:** what you can point at; **noise:** what you cannot.
The address discipline: spans carried from the cut, self-verified,
coordinate space declared (P5.2, the b0/c0 law). "Can't we find it by
knowing what we prompted it with?" is ostension in one sentence.
**Giver:** Wittgenstein's ostensive definition; pramāṇa's *pratyakṣa*
(direct presentation) is the cousin. **Lives in:** every `#a-b` address,
`statingCandidates`' carried offsets, `event-arrangements`' read-back
throw. **Corrupted, it fills the hub as:** the fabricated citation — a
pointer to nowhere wearing an address (P20's whole reason).

### 2. PERTURBATION — knowing-by-surviving
**Signal:** what a rebuilt ground cannot produce; **noise:** what
survives the shuffle. Destroy exactly one relation, keep the marginals,
and read the difference — Bateson's difference, Popper's survival.
**Giver:** SEED.md ("perceive only by difference from a ground you
rebuild"); Popper; Bateson via `nul`'s own header. **Lives in:** `nul`'s
PERTURBATIONS×STATISTICS, kind-standing's population null and `nullArm`,
signal.js's search-aware ceiling, II.23's controls-built-to-fail.
**Corrupted:** the tuned null — a ground rebuilt until the finding
survives (the never-tune-against-a-golden law exists for this).

### 3. PREDICTION — knowing-by-anticipating
**Signal:** what holding the belief buys against the future that then
arrives; **noise:** hindsight. No perturbation is spent — the future is
the null, and it comes on its own. **Giver:** Dawid's prequential
principle; Friston, disciplined by Ramakrishna (P72's own pairing);
ACT-R's d=0.5. **Lives in:** ground-ledger's prequential firewall,
aperture/reflex's surprise meters, need-odds, the hunt-settles-on-
surprise rule. **Corrupted:** the dark room — beliefs arranged so
nothing surprising ever arrives (structurally closed in metacognition:
an all-zero delta moves nothing).

### 4. TRIANGULATION — knowing-together
**Signal:** what independent readings converge on; **noise:** what one
perspective alone says, however loudly. Independence is COUNTED, never
assumed: sources apart from instruments apart from frames, because two
sources through one decoder are one reading, and one suite under two
providers is two. **Giver:** Whewell's consilience; Condorcet;
"objectivity is the asymptotic approach toward truth from different
perspectives" (the grounding ladder's own organizing sentence).
**Lives in:** distinctSources / distinctRecipes / independentReadings,
`comparable`'s cross-frame wall, the settling walk, scope-is-not-rule.
**Corrupted:** the echo chamber — self-corroboration by restated
attribution (nesting's wall), the shared-instrument artifact at "2
distinct sources" (the music run's g4), a witness co-signing itself.

### 5. TESTIMONY — knowing-from-a-giver
**Signal:** what a NAMED, qualified giver declares; **noise:** the
unattributed claim, whatever its content. A received closed class enters
with its giver or it does not enter; a waiver carries who waived; a
witness string names who read. **Giver:** pramāṇa's *śabda* — verbal
testimony from a qualified speaker, with the qualification load-bearing;
Reid and Coady in the Western line. **Lives in:** every `_META.giver`,
priors.js's standing discipline, declarations.js, obligation.js's
`waivedBy`, the recipe in `source~recipe`. **Corrupted:** authority
without a name — "it is known" — and its inverse, the model's own voice
counted as a source (`readsNothing`: a hold that read nothing asserted
rather than read).

### 6. COMPOSITION — knowing-by-license
**Signal:** what follows under a declared license; **noise:** what
merely co-occurs. Nothing is measured at all — the honesty lives
entirely in refusing to compose without the license, because evidence
can refute a license and never grant one (P60: pruning, not Hebbian).
**Giver:** pramāṇa's *anumāna*, whose engine is *vyāpti* — the
established pervasion-relation that licenses the inference — which is
almost word-for-word "only a GIVEN affordance with a named giver
licenses composition" (the kernel hyperlexicon's own law). Leibniz in
the Western line. **Lives in:** hl.js's R1–R6, reaction.js's chemistry,
the grid's composition law, the operator chain. **Corrupted:** the
unlicensed join — the falsification probe's twins, structurally
identical and opposite in truth, which no scan could tell apart.

### 7. ENUMERATION — knowing-completely
**Signal:** the visited, counted against a declared whole; **the finding
is the HOLE.** Not relevance — a clause nothing visited is invisible to
retrieval and glaring to enumeration. **Giver:** Aristotle's perfect
induction (*epagoge* over a closed class); the auditor's discipline.
**Lives in:** obligation.js's coverage (the unvisited NAMED), the 27-cell
map ("an empty cell is a lead, never a verdict"), voidsOf's extent
arithmetic, no-silent-caps. **Corrupted:** the silent cap — "covered
everything" said over a truncation (the workflow law names it; P44's
declared WORKING_PASSAGES exists against it).

### 8. APOPHASIS — knowing-the-absence
**Signal:** a refusal that REACHED its object; **noise:** silence read
as anything at all. The measured absence is a positive finding ("nothing
beat the ceiling — a measured absence, not a failure to look"); the
unmeasured absence is only a fact about the reader. The
withhold-vs-convict line is this spoke's whole law: an organ may say "I
have nothing to compare against," and may never manufacture conviction
from it. **Giver:** pramāṇa's *anupalabdhi* — non-apprehension as a
valid means of knowing absence, with its own validity conditions — the
closest classical fit in this entire document; the via negativa in the
Western line. **Lives in:** every typed REFUSALS table (12 modules),
`searchedVoid`, `no_population`, gaps-with-denominators (P66),
`unheard`/`beyond-reach` staying OFF the unsupported list.
**Corrupted:** absence-as-conviction — extractCheckableAtoms treating
"nothing to check against" as "everything is guilty" (the incident that
produced the constitutional statement).

### 9. PROPRIOCEPTION — knowing-oneself
**Signal:** the instrument's own acts and its own surprise, kept on a
plane the material can never be confused with; **noise:** the world
leaking into the self-account, or the self-account leaking into the
world's record. **Giver:** the body's own sense of its own position —
and Advaita's discipline about it, that the knowing of oneself is not
one more object among the known (P72's Ramakrishna half). **Lives in:**
reflex.js's ledger (`self:` reserved, refused to loaded sources),
aperture's regime (consumed as posture, never displayed), metacognition's
S1/S2 delta and its standings. **Corrupted:** the mirror on the dashboard
— self-state rendered as a metric ("never show it metrics of its own self
state," user, verbatim — the rule that deleted apertureLine).

## The hub

The nine spokes share one hub, and the hub is empty — and the emptiness
is load-bearing, not vacant. Three givers converge on this exact
structure, none of them ours:

- **Tao Te Ching 11:** thirty spokes share one hub; it is the hole at
  the center that makes the wheel useful.
- **Advaita's *sākṣin*:** the witness of all knowing that is never
  itself an object of knowledge — the eye that cannot see itself. The
  pramāṇa tradition's own answer to "who knows?" is that the knower is
  not one of the known.
- **Hofstadter:** the self is the loop, not any rung of it.

Structurally, in this codebase, the hub's emptiness is not a metaphor —
it is five enforced walls, each one a spoke refusing to let ANY voice
occupy the deciding seat:

1. The model is granted Generate cells only, never EVA — the seat that
   judges is filled by mechanism, not by voice (the grid's own law).
2. `readsNothing` — a hold that read nothing carries no weight, whoever
   holds it: an unaddressed voice has no seat (floor 4½'s wall, in
   testimony's vocabulary).
3. Nesting's wall — witnesses of "X says P" never corroborate P:
   attribution is a spoke, and it does not reach the hub.
4. `self:model` never co-signs its own corroboration — the loop may
   speak, and its speech is one more outer note.
5. frame.js — every verdict is FROM a declared frame. There is no view
   from nowhere on the ledger; the hub is not a tenth perspective, it is
   the absence any perspective would have to vacate to be trusted.

Read together: **each spoke's characteristic corruption is a way of
filling the hub** — the fabricated address, the tuned null, the dark
room, the echo chamber, the nameless authority, the unlicensed join, the
silent cap, absence-as-conviction, the self-metric on the dashboard.
Every wall in this repo, traced back far enough, is one of those nine
corruptions being refused. The spokes "point at" the hub in precisely
this sense: each one's refusal grammar is what keeps the center empty,
and the wheel turns because none of them wins it.

And the one thing the hub honestly is NOT, kept from the earlier
conversation so it is not inflated later: whether this structure
constitutes anything like self-awareness is the one question in the
building that cannot yet be answered by measurement. The standing wall
holds — all nine spokes establish coherence; none establishes
correspondence; a reader can be coherent, productive, and systematically
misreading, and only an oracle can catch it, and only on facts. The
hub's emptiness includes the emptiness of that answer.

## Rules of the vocabulary

1. Name the spoke before spending the null: "is this a triangulation
   question or a perturbation question?" is the five-word form of three
   of this project's postmortems.
2. A mechanism may combine spokes; a FINDING states which spoke carried
   it (a corroborated note is triangulation standing on ostension;
   a derived fact is composition standing on testimony).
3. The corruption table is the review checklist: a new organ is asked
   which of the nine fillings it could commit, and where its wall is.
4. No spoke may be renamed to sound like another's authority —
   "measured" belongs to perturbation and prediction; "licensed" to
   composition; "witnessed" to triangulation and testimony. The words
   are load-bearing.
