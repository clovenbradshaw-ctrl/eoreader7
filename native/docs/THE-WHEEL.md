## The one act, three positions

Not three things. Void / Beings / Fold is a single event seen from three
stands:

1. **The void is the nothing against which** — the rebuilt ground, the
   field the reading stands on, re-hollowed by every REC. NUL (`THE-NULL-
   STATES.md`, `kernel/fold.js::receivedGround`): the empty count that
   opens the chain. The witness measures its openness, never its fullness
   (`kernel/ground-closure.js`).
2. **The being is the difference against it** — a figure admitted against
   the void: a referent born at first admission (S80: an address is a
   birth, not a spelling), a span-free node (`EOReferent@1`, S113).
3. **The fold is the difference the being MADE** — the change the figure
   wrought on the next ground: Bateson's difference-that-makes-a-
   difference, the Pattern grain (`THE-THREE-MATHEMATICS.md`), the
   re-formed prior that primes the next read. The SEED rows exactly:
   Ground (constant), Figure (value), Pattern (rate).

## The arithmetic: 0 / n / 1

The void holds no count (0). The beings are the many (n). The fold is one
(1). **From nothing, the many; from the many, the one.** Involution and
evolution in one stroke — the same 0 → n → 1 the seed's rows already run,
named in the wheel's own terms.

## The walls, named once

- **The void must stay empty to work.** A filled void is a jammed wheel.
  Rebuilding the ground is re-hollowing the hub, never repairing a defect —
  the witness's one job, re-ground not accumulate (`kernel/ground-closure.js`).
- **A being is the fold at a point** — void behind, fold ahead; the spoke
  is a gathering, never an atom. A referent is a whole to its canonical
  occurrences, a part to its kind (THE-ADDRESS §4).
- **The fold is never the sum of the beings** — it is what they make
  possible. Coherence is never correspondence (THE-ADDRESS §8, the standing
  wall: hashing is a way of keeping, never a way of knowing).
- **Analogy with a giver** (this naming is a pointing, never a proof);
  **convergence not proof**; **numerology as flag not evidence** — 0/n/1 is
  a mnemonic for the seed's rows, never an argument for them.
