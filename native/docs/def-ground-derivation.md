## The cell and its siblings

DEF·Ground — **Clearing at Atmosphere**: defining the interpretive
ground. Its depth-siblings on the stance face (Clearing = Differentiate ×
Ground, one per mathematics):

- **NUL·Ground** (arithmetic — `measure`): clear the ZERO. measure.js's
  `admit` is a declaration gate — a measurement whose numbers (draws,
  window, seed) are not declared is REFUSED, typed, never defaulted,
  because an undeclared number silently means two different things before
  and after more material arrives.
- **SEG·Ground** (geometry — `extent`): clear the EXTENT — the space to
  be covered AND ITS UNITS, made operative. Same shape: a coverage claim
  without declared units is not a claim.

## The derivation

Both siblings are the same machine: a **declaration gate with typed
refusals**, converting "the caller must say X" from prose into a wall,
plus a rule that two results are COMPARABLE only when their declarations
match. Arithmetic's version guards numbers; geometry's guards extent and
units. The calculus version, by direct transposition, must guard **the
reference frame of judgment**: which organs, givers, and numbers were IN
FORCE when a verdict was computed.

Predicted design, in full, before building:

1. `declareFrame({organs, givers, numbers})` → a frozen frame with a
   content-addressed id. Refuses, typed, when an organ is unnamed, a
   giver is absent, or a number carries no value (`undeclared_frame`,
   naming exactly what is missing — measure.js's own refusal grammar).
2. Verdicts CARRY their frame id, the way measure results carry their
   declaration and witnesses carry their recipe (P68).
3. `comparable(a, b)` → two verdicts from DIFFERENT frames REFUSE
   comparison, typed (`cross_frame`), naming the components that differ.
   Same frame → comparison allowed. This is the calculus-specific
   content: comparing verdicts across undeclared frames is the classic
   sin (a p-value against a different alpha; a `bound` under a different
   vocabulary), and no organ in this repo currently walls it.

## The specimen, already measured — the plan's own gate is met

CAPACITY-DEVELOPMENT-PLAN's gate: *"What would a SPECIMEN even be — a
turn whose interpretive ground is definably different with the
definition absent? Until that question has a measured answer, this cell
stays honestly empty."*

The answer arrived 2026-09-01/02, live: `hypergraph.test.mjs` had been
verifying the FROZEN legacy provider while app.js runs native — the same
suite measured **54/58 under one frame and 52/58 under the other**, and
the difference was invisible for as long as the frame was undeclared.
The fix was literally a frame declaration (`ENGINE=native`), after which
the gap became "a measurement anyone can take instead of a surprise."
That is a turn whose interpretive ground was definably different with
the definition absent — the cell's absence produced a live incident
after all, closing the plan's own "never produced one" note.

## The falsifiable part

The build that follows must succeed USING ONLY the design above — a
declaration gate with typed refusals, carried frame ids, and a
cross-frame comparison wall — exercised against the real specimen (the
two engine providers as two frames over the same material). If that
design proves insufficient and something structurally different is
needed, §VIII.2 loses the point and THE-THREE-MATHEMATICS says so.
