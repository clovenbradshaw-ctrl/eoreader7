# Capacity development plan — the eight cells still empty after the connection

Companion to `CAPABILITY-POLICIES.md` (the map and its laws) and P64 (the
connection pass that paid the registry debt). State as of 2026-08-29,
measured by `eval/capability-coverage.mjs`: **19/27 cells registered, no
operator at zero, no empty stance; eight cells empty.** This document is
the per-cell plan for those eight — what candidate organ exists (where one
was found by the same hunt that paid the debt), what typing it would need
confirmed, and what falsifiable specimen its registration must carry.

**The three rules every step below is under**, so they are not re-derived
per cell:

1. **A cell is filled by an organ plus a specimen, never by a row alone**
   (CAPABILITY C5.3; P41 — a check that did not run must never report a
   pass). CON·Pattern is the template: its emptiness was confirmed by a
   stated-then-observed prediction, and its organ (`network.js`) landed
   with tests against that same specimen class.
2. **Nominations are not registrations.** A candidate organ named below
   enters the registry only after its cell typing is confirmed — either
   located as documented in the organ's own code/header, or reasoned and
   then mechanically domain-checked, the registry's own original
   discipline. An eager typing is exactly the mistake `capacities.js`'s
   header records catching twice.
3. **Search for the organ before building one** (this project's standing
   rule). Half the "empty" map turned out to be unregistered organs; each
   cell below records how far its hunt has gone, so the next pass extends
   the hunt rather than repeating it.

## Tier 1 — named candidates already in this repo (confirm typing, add specimen, register)

### SIG·Ground — Tending at Void: naming what is absent

**Candidates, both real and running:** `holon.js`'s `searchedVoid` /
`SEARCHED_VOID_PREFIX` (P32's companion: a preflight search that ran and
found nothing is FED TO THE MODEL as an acknowledged void — signing the
absence so it is never mistaken for an unattempted search), and
`void-loop.js::whatWouldSettle` (P53's second amendment: a gap the loop
can name is a question it can ask — turning loop state into the named
questions that would settle it). Both are acts of NAMING an absence,
which is what Relate·Existence at Ground grain reads as.

**To confirm:** neither carries a documented cell typing; the SIG·Ground
reading is this plan's nomination. **Specimen:** a search that genuinely
found nothing must produce a typed, model-visible void acknowledgement
(already measured live in P32's amendment — re-run as the registration's
own specimen), and a loop holding an unplaced filler must emit the
specific question that would place it (P53's Johnson specimen, already
measured).

### SIG·Pattern — Tracing at Kind: signing a recurring kind

**Candidate:** the engine's kind-induction pair — `induceKinds` plus its
null arm (`emergence/kinds.js` + `host/terrains.js::kindsNullArm`,
eoreader6.1) — already live in Explore's kinds view, already carrying the

**To confirm:** the typing (nominated, not documented), and the PATH —

Registering a legacy-engine module is a real decision (Constitution I.2
adjacency), not a formality. **Specimen already exists:** the

### NUL·Pattern — Unraveling at Kind: null-testing a kind

## Tier 2 — plausible candidates needing real design work before nomination is honest

### CON·Ground — Tending at Field: maintaining the connective ground

**Sketch:** the running summary machinery (`fold.js`'s S1 +
`refreshSummary`/`advanceSummaryFold`) MAINTAINS the conversational
field — a Relate·Structure act at Ground grain. But the aperture gate
that DECIDES whether ground is carried is already registered (EVA·Ground,
`atmosphere`), and splitting maintain-the-ground from judge-the-ground
needs a cleaner boundary than a nomination can draw from outside those
modules (both are the fold-architecture session's contract). **Do not
register without that session's own boundary.**

### DEF·Ground — Clearing at Atmosphere: defining the interpretive ground

**Sketch:** the constitution's channel (`constitution.js`'s
CONSTITUTION_PROMPT — the one folded paragraph that sets every turn's
interpretive ambient) and the compiled-priors load (`loadCompiledPriors`
— the standing experiential ground admitted at a gate) are both
candidates; neither typing is obvious enough to nominate without a
worked case. What would a SPECIMEN even be — a turn whose interpretive
ground is definably different with the definition absent? Until that
question has a measured answer, this cell stays honestly empty. It is
also the cell whose absence has never produced a live incident — worth
noting, since every organ in this repo was built against one.

### INS·Ground — Cultivating at Void: generating ground where none exists

**Sketch:** P23's preflight (`gatherPreflightMaterial` — a materialless
grounded turn gets one search BEFORE drafting, generating the ground the
check needs) is a real, measured mechanism whose act reads as
Generate·Existence at Ground. It lives in `app.js` (the
fold-architecture session's contract), so registration waits on the same
boundary as CON·Ground. The alternative reading (fresh-log constructors
as ground-birth) is too thin to be worth a row.

### SEG·Pattern — Unraveling at Network: cutting a pattern apart

**The only cell with no candidate anywhere** — nothing in either repo
decomposes a Network: no community separation over the belief graph, no
edge-set removal that leaves two readable sub-networks, no unpicking of a
recurring arrangement into its constituent runs. Nearest neighbours are
all other acts: `refuteRelation` finds what to distrust (EVA/DEF-shaped),
`withdraw` takes back products (REC), `atmosphereBoundaries` cuts a
STREAM (SEG at Field, arguably — but it segments a linear ground, not a
network). **Specimen to build against, already in the corpus:** a
two-topic document whose belief graph should separate into two
components at the same boundary `atmosphereBoundaries` finds in the
stream — a cut whose two halves each still read, with the cut edges
disclosed. This is the genuine frontier cell, and per CAPABILITY C4 it
stays a LEAD (no falsifiable emptiness-prediction has been stated for
it) until someone states one or builds the organ.

1. **NUL·Figure** (Tier 1) — the plan is P22's own named next
   integration, the specimen is staged, and it hardens `distinguish` for

   measured specimens; the only work is the typing confirmation and the

3. **SIG·Pattern + NUL·Pattern together** (Tier 1) — one decision (the
   legacy-engine path question) unlocks both; they complete Tracing to
   3/3 and bring Unraveling to 2/3.
4. **SEG·Pattern** (Tier 2) — the real build. Completes Unraveling.
5. **CON·Ground / INS·Ground / DEF·Ground** — gated on the
   fold-architecture session's boundary (two of three) and on a workable
   specimen (the third). Completing these completes the map.

A completed map, restated so nobody inflates it (CAPABILITY C7): 27/27
would mean every KIND of act the received algebra names is performable
somewhere — not that any is performed correctly (coherence < 
correspondence; the oracle rule stands), and not that each organ reaches
every ORDER of task (the MHC axis is separate, and pairing it per cell
is the map's own named future work).
