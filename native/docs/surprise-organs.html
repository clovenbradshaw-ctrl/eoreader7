What a reading learns

One book, read once by the constitutional reader and never re-read here. This page walks that record forward, sentence by sentence, and asks four questions of it as it goes: how much each sentence changed what the reader expected, and how much of that landed on beings the record leans on; who holds the stage, and for how long; what was said before the reader knew two names were one; and whose identity the reader kept changing its mind about. Every number is an organ's own return value. Identities are as they stood at each point — a merge counts only from the sentence where the reader recorded it.
