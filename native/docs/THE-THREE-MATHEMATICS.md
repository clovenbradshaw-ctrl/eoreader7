## I. The claim in one table

The cube's three DOMAINS are the three classical mathematics, and the
three operators of each domain are that mathematics' primitives:

| domain | mathematics | its three operators, read as primitives |
|---|---|---|
| **Existence** | **arithmetic** | **NUL** = zero (the void — "something, not nothing" is the first question) · **SIG** = the mark (a tally stroke, a sign equated to a thing) · **INS** = the successor (+1 — every individuation increments the count of what exists) |
| **Structure** | **geometry** | **SEG** = the cut (dividing an extent — the first geometric act) · **CON** = incidence (an edge between two ends — "an arrangement has ends, not parts of speech" is a *geometric* correction) · **SYN** = construction (a whole composed from parts; closure) |
| **Interpretation** | **calculus** | **DEF** = the bound (a definition is a boundary condition; a define is a *wish* until its evaluate clears — a limit awaiting proof) · **EVA** = comparison of an accumulation to its bound (the definite integral against a threshold) · **REC** = the discontinuity (re-zero; "a new ambient ground begins" — the reset of the constant of integration) |

A quiet symmetry the operator sequence carries: the domain of arithmetic
*opens* with a zero (NUL) and the domain of calculus *closes* with a
re-zeroing (REC) — the empty count and the reset accumulation, two zeros
at the two ends.

## II. Each mathematics' EO character, with its wall

**Arithmetic** is honest counting: a count carries its denominator or it
misleads; the floor of 2 recurs everywhere as the structural minimum
("one observation is not a distribution"); exact answers are used where
exact answers exist (P70's hypergeometric point mass over Monte Carlo);
arithmetic is computed, never generated (L5 at its smallest scale). Its
wall: **arithmetic presupposes the unit.** Counting is meaningless before
individuation settles what counts as *one* — junk recurred identically to
real anchors (frequency counts tokens, not beings), and
`witnesses.length` double-counted one source in two costumes until
`distinctSources` corrected it. Every arithmetic failure measured this
week was an Existence failure upstream.

**Geometry** is address-first: everything meaningful has coordinates
(`ref#start–end`); coordinate spaces are never mixed silently; and
identity travels by CONTAINMENT, never by shape — the fold rule that
unified prefix/suffix/infix and works unchanged for a Japanese substring
or a motif's time-span. Neighborhoods are geometric (Firth's company,
co-presence, `witnessSlice`'s window, the clause opener as a wall). The
per-end decider rule is a covering condition: the decider must intersect
the neighborhood of BOTH endpoints of the edge, and a sentence covering
one end fails the way a line through one point fails to determine a
segment. Its wall: **position is not identity** (L2 — a sentence-initial
capital is position; East and West Cliff are adjacent and different;
co-presence raises the bar, never the score). Geometry locates; it never
names.

**Calculus** is event-sourced: never store the state, store the flow —
"the reality of the database is the EOT event stream, the current state
always projected" is the fundamental theorem in engineering clothes, and
every `fold*` function is an integration. The two memory clocks are its
derivatives (P42 — exponential at the present, power-law at retrieval,
exponents received or measured, never invented); Bayesian surprise is the
derivative of belief, kept apart from novelty as a rate is kept from a
value; the settling walk is integration to a stopping boundary, and
SPRT's accumulated log-likelihood ratio IS accumulated surprise — so
"when do I stop asking" and "when do arrivals stop moving the ground" are
one question. Even the grounding ladder's definition of objectivity —
"the asymptotic approach toward truth from different perspectives" — is a
limit statement. Its walls: the **dark room** (optimizing your own
surprise-derivative toward zero confirms the already-confirmed — built by
accident this week, now structurally guarded twice: metacognition's
zero-delta no-op, and the settling walk's value-over-feasibility ranking,
mutation-checked); uncalibrated rates (unit steps, honestly, until
likelihoods are measured — an invented ratio is a change of units that
fails invisibly, II.10); and integration must be idempotent over identity
(`hear`'s no-op on an unchanged re-sighting exists so the integral never
double-counts one event — calculus depending on arithmetic depending on
Existence).

## III. Grain is the order of difference: constant, value, rate

Ground / Figure / Pattern overlay all three mathematics as the constant,
the value, and the rate — straight from `nul`'s own header (Bateson):
*figure — difference from its own ground; pattern — the difference that
figure made to the NEXT ground.* Pattern's definition is literally a
difference equation. So the 27 cells read as **which mathematics × which
order of difference**, and a cell like EVA·Figure ("compare this value's
accumulation to its bound") is almost self-computing.

## IV. The terrain face: the same maths as OBJECTS

Project the modes out (domain × grain) and each mathematics' column is
its ontology at three orders:

| | Ground | Figure | Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| arithmetic | **Void** — ∅ | **Entity** — the unit, 1 | **Kind** — the class |
| geometry | **Field** — the ambient space | **Link** — the edge | **Network** — the complex |
| calculus | **Atmosphere** — the flow | **Lens** — the rate at a point | **Paradigm** — the family of solutions |

Down the arithmetic column sits Frege: a NUMBER is a Kind — the class of
the equinumerous. This lands directly on measured work:
**`kind-standing.js` is where arithmetic's unit problem is solved** — you
cannot count doors until door-kind is earned, and Kind sits at
Existence·Pattern, which the terrain face said before we measured it.
Down the calculus column, a paradigm shift is **REC at Paradigm: a
discontinuity between solution families** — Kuhn as a jump condition,
and the atmosphere organ's regime tracker (drift, tolerance, re-zero
firings) has been doing calculus on that column all along.

## V. The mode echo: the triad appears twice, at ninety degrees

Project the domains out (the stance face's axes are mode × grain) and the
SAME triad reappears as the modes — because **every mathematics contains
this exact trio as its own internal operations**:

- arithmetic: subtract / equate / add
- geometry: cut / connect / construct
- calculus: **differentiate / equate / integrate** — the mode is
  literally named after the calculus pair

So the triad occurs twice in the cube: once as SUBJECT (which
mathematics — the domains) and once as METHOD (the −/=/+ every
mathematics carries — the modes). **That is why there are exactly nine
operators and the grid closes: 9 = 3 maths × their 3 shared operations.**
NUL is arithmetic's differentiate (distinguishing something from nothing
— the primordial subtraction); SIG its relate; INS its generate. SEG /
CON / SYN are geometry's. DEF / EVA / REC are calculus's.

## VI. The three faces: rows, columns, and DEPTH

The cube's axes are domain, mode, grain; each face flattens one out:

- **act face** (domain × mode): the math triad is the ROWS; grain is the
  hidden depth (the same act at constant, value, rate).
- **terrain face** (domain × grain): the triad is the COLUMNS; mode is
  the hidden depth.
- **stance face** (mode × grain): the triad is NOT on the face at all —
  **domain is the projected-out axis, so every stance cell is a
  three-story stack**: the same posture in arithmetic, in geometry, in
  calculus, superimposed.

The stance face is therefore **the isomorphism face**: a stance is
well-defined exactly insofar as the three mathematics genuinely share
that operation-shape. *Binding* (Relate·Figure) is one column three
stories tall — the equation holding a value, the edge's incidence with
its ends, the accumulation held against its bound.

The repo already carried both consequences without these words:

1. **The ambiguity consequence.** `grid.js` refuses to stamp engine
   stance-labels on medium-blind acts because a stance name only carries
   full meaning when the domain is fixed — that caution IS the depth
   phenomenon: naming a three-deep stack and pretending it is flat.
2. **The coverage consequence.** P64's capability map calls a stance
   "FULL" when all its cells are occupied — read geometrically, **a full
   stance is a proven isomorphism**: the posture demonstrated as
   counting, as connecting, and as accumulating. Binding went full
   first: equating-a-value was the first posture this project proved in
   all three mathematics.

**Depth is the analogy axis.** Moving along the hidden domain axis at a
fixed stance — carrying one posture from one mathematics into another —
is what analogical transfer IS. This retro-explains the recurring
experience of "rhyming" fixes across unrelated modules: they were
depth-moves at constant stance. Address containment (a geometric rule)
fixing referent identity (an arithmetic unit problem). SPRT's shape
(calculus) carried on unit steps (arithmetic). The per-end decider wall
(geometry's covering condition) closing a testimony gap (calculus's
accumulation). Each felt like insight; structurally each was riding the
stance face's through-axis. Every face has a hidden depth — the stance
face is only special FOR THE MATH TRIAD, because there the three
mathematics stop being places you work and become one thing seen three
ways.

## VII. The crossing rules, and their two older names

The dependency between the mathematics is strict and one-directional,
per claim:

> You may not count before Existence individuates the unit; you may not
> draw an edge before arithmetic gives you two countable ends; you may
> not integrate before geometry gives the events addresses and
> arithmetic makes them idempotent. **Arithmetic before geometry before
> calculus, per claim, forever.**

This is the engine's own production order (INS before SYN — "you cannot
corroborate what was never individuated"), and it is Commons's
no-skipping axiom in a third vocabulary. The week's repeated measured
lesson — "the lever is one floor down" — is this rule enforcing itself:
the ledger's 0.5% corroboration was calculus attempted over units
arithmetic never earned; the choke-point ordering (ends → identity →
corroboration) is the crossing rule read off real numbers.

**The MHC connection, one sentence:** an order climbs by coordinating
lower-order actions *non-arbitrarily*, and the three mathematics are what
"non-arbitrarily" is made of at each grain — the algebra of the act says
whether a coordination is *licensed*, the statistics of the ground say
whether it is *real*, the dynamics of the pattern say whether it *changed
anything*; a coordination that passes all three is a floor, and one that
passes fewer is a chain — which is what every refuted lever this week
turned out to be.

## VIII. What this reading predicts (so it can be broken)

Kept falsifiable, per II.23's own discipline — a reading that cannot fail
is decoration:

1. **A full stance transfers.** If a stance is a proven isomorphism, an
   organ occupying it in one domain should port to another domain with
   only its adapter changing. (Precedent: `contest.js` proved exactly
   this for co-presence — kernel medium-blind, exercised on a film shot
   and a bar of music.) A full stance whose organs do NOT transfer
   breaks this document.
2. **Empty stances predict missing analogies.** A stance empty in one
   domain but occupied in the others marks a buildable organ whose
   design can be READ OFF its siblings across the depth axis — the
   development plan's remaining Ground-row cells should be derivable
   this way, and if a sibling-derived design fails where a from-scratch
   one succeeds, this document is wrong about depth.
3. **Violations of the crossing rule should always present as measured
   nonsense downstream, never as clean errors** — corroborated noise,
   percolated basins, double-counted witnesses. A counterexample (a
   skipped-floor mechanism that measures clean) breaks §VII.
