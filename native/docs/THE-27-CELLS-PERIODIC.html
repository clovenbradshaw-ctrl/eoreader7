The 27 cells — periodic table

The space is 27 = 9 operators × 3 grains; mode, domain, terrain and stance are all derived from that pair — there is no third free axis. Cells classify moves, never content. Rows are the grains (the size of the difference); columns are the operators in canonical chain order (NUL SIG INS SEG CON SYN DEF EVA REC), grouped into the three domain blocks.

Color = mode (what the act does): ■ Differentiate — cut apart · ■ Relate — put beside · ■ Generate — bring into being. Each cell carries three faces: the organ (the registered act), the archon (the who — the named discipline it is performed under, from the README Handle table), and the exemplar (the what — the most discriminative real clause landing in that cell, from the eo-lexical-analysis-2.0 runs; “no clean exemplar” means the study found none).
