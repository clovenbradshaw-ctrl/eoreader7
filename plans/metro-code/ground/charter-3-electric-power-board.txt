Sec. 11.301. - Electric Power Board of Nashville continued as metropolitan agency.
The Electric Power Board of Nashville, created and established by chapter 246, Private Acts of 1947, and amendatory acts, the same being the Charter of the City of Nashville, shall continue to exist as the Electric Power Board of the Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County and to function as an agency of the metropolitan government, and shall have all the rights, duties, powers, obligations, privileges and responsibilities as are contained in the terms and provisions of said Private Acts or Municipal Charter. A copy of the terms and provisions of said Private Acts or Municipal Charter, with modifications limited to those changes necessary and proper to constitute said electric power board an agency of the metropolitan government, is attached to this Charter and made a part hereof as Appendix Three hereto.
